Sensor Harmony

Understanding MODIS and VIIRS fire detections

MODIS

Fire detection resolution: approximately 1 km.

Satellites: Terra and Aqua.

Provides a long historical record of fire activity.

VIIRS

Fire detection resolution: approximately 375 m.

Satellite: Suomi NPP (dataset used here).

Can detect smaller hot spots than MODIS.

Why harmonization matters

MODIS and VIIRS have different spatial resolutions, sensitivities, and observation times. Their detection counts are not directly equivalent and should not simply be added together.

Current prototype: sensor-specific daily counts and comparisons. Full cross-sensor harmonization remains future work.
